Gear Box System · design pages

The boxes

Printable gear boxes on a 64 mm grid. Each one passes motion through gears in its walls: in on the West and South, out on the East and North. Every box has a page with its assembled and exploded views, ports, torque cost, parts, assembly and what it teaches.

51 fit-checked · none printed yet · budgets: crank 20 stages, motor 15

drive input port output port display inner gears structure

Try a layout before you print it: the sandbox puts boxes on the grid, turns the crank and moves every gear, and flags lanes that miss, undriven channels, ratchet jams, the torque budget and backlash. BEAT, BOOM and ROLL play out loud, and the 29 kit builds load one kit at a time.

Print them: every box and kit will have its print files on MakerWorld. See the MakerWorld list.

The rules every box follows are in the box standard. The bigger picture is in What the gear boxes can do.

Each family heading below carries its lid color: the family colors the lid, and the role colors above stay the same on every box. See Family colors.

Where to start: the kits group the fit-checked boxes into six sets, a starter and five that add to it, each with builds to try, the filament it takes and what to buy.

Carry a layout: the deck is a printed base the boxes snap down onto, so a whole layout lifts as one piece. In the standard: every shell has a socket under each grid node, checked on a deck by the fit check; not printed yet.

Three plots, one motor: three PLOT boxes drawn from one DRIVE, laid out two ways: 26 boxes with today's parts, or an 11-box tower with PLOT's time on W and the new BRANCH and long wires.

Drives and inputs

Where motion comes from: a hand, a motor, a press, a spring.

CRANK ↻

fit-checked

Hand drive: a thumb wheel with a crank knob. Drives E and N forward.

1 cell · budget 20

MOTOR ⚡

fit-checked

9 V gearmotor drive, two cells wide: forward, off and reverse, ports at about 45 rpm.

2×1 cells · budget 15

SPRING ➰

fit-checked

A clockwork motor: wind the red knob a turn, lift the plunger and a printed spring drives E; let go and it stops.

1 cell · budget 2

PUSH · FLIP · CLICK ⏵

fit-checked

A drive you press: a red key pushes a rack, one full press is one port turn. FLIP adds a flipper bat, CLICK a ratchet tally.

1 cell · budget 12 press, 4 return

Routing

Carry a line across the grid, turn it, split it, lift it.

WIRE →

fit-checked

Straight through on both channels: W→E and S→N.

1 cell · cost 2

TURN ↰

fit-checked

W in, N out: turns the main line into the second channel.

1 cell · cost 3

TAP ⤷

fit-checked

S in, E out: restarts a row from the N/S power line, at ×1.

1 cell · cost 3

TEE ⊤

fit-checked

W in, E and N out: the simplest fan-out.

1 cell · cost 3

BUS ≡

fit-checked

Two cells tall: the power line in on S, copied out east on both rows and passed on north.

1×2 cells · cost 3 (5 to E2 and N)

RISER ⇡

fit-checked

Two cells tall: in on W of the lower cell, out on E of the upper one.

1×2 cells · cost 4

WIRE 2 · WIRE 3 · POLE 2 · POLE 3 ═

fit-checked

WIRE, two or three cells long. WIRE 2 and 3 carry the main line east, POLE 2 and 3 carry the power line north (and let rows cross anywhere), so a long run is one box, not a row of single WIREs.

2×1, 3×1, 1×2, 1×3 cells · cost 2 a cell

BRANCH ⊢

fit-checked

TAP with a north port: the power line comes up from the south, leaves east into the row, and goes on north, so one drive can start a row in every cell of a column.

1 cell · cost 3 (E), 3 (on north)

Switches and clocks

Choose which way the line goes, and make the timing signals that choose it.

ROUTE ⇄

fit-checked

A switch on the lid: turn the knob and the line leaves on E or on N. A swinging tumbler gear, break before make.

1 cell · cost 3

PICK ⇉

fit-checked

ROUTE turned round: the knob chooses whether W or S drives E.

1 cell · cost 3

STEER ⑂

fit-checked

A second line on S chooses where the line goes: high to E, low to N. Drive it from a SQUARE clock. Replaces the planned selector and stick shift.

1 cell · cost 3 (+2 select)

SWAP ↯

fit-checked

A different output every turn: E, N, E, N. A T flip-flop in one box: a Geneva step and a cam throw STEER's tumbler.

1×2 cells · cost 3 + 5

SQUARE ⊓

fit-checked

A gear clock: N steps between low and high once a cycle. Turn one cam plate a click to set the duty, 33 to 92 %.

1 cell · cost about 4

RAMP ⋀

fit-checked

SQUARE's frame with heart cams: a triangle wave, or slow up and quick down, to a sawtooth.

1 cell · cost about 4

Motion shapers

Turn steady rotation into steps, one-way motion, lifts and slides.

GENEVA ⊞

fit-checked

One turn in, a quarter turn out, then held still: a Geneva step.

1 cell · cost 3

RATCHET ⇥

fit-checked

W→E forward only: a pawl stops the line running back. Push its tab to release.

1 cell · cost 3

CAM ⌒

fit-checked

W turns a face cam; a follower rod rises and falls 0 to 8 mm through the lid.

1 cell · cost 2

YOKE ∿

fit-checked

A Scotch yoke: W's turning becomes a slider moving 20 mm side to side.

1 cell · cost 2

FLYWHEEL ◉

fit-checked

W→E at x1 through a 48 mm disc that keeps the line turning after you let go.

1 cell · cost 3

CLUTCH ⊜

fit-checked

W→E at x1 through a slip clutch: a red knob sets how hard it grips, and past that the line stops while the input runs on.

1 cell · cost 3

TRIP ⚑

fit-checked

A one-shot flag: a cam bump lifts it, a sprung fork on the lid holds it up until you push the reset tab.

1 cell · cost 2

TICK ⏱

fit-checked

An escapement: a foliot on the lid lets the line through one tooth per swing, tick, tock.

1 cell · cost 2

GOVERNOR ⊛

fit-checked

A speed limit: a rotor on the lid, geared up 6.25 times, whose shoes fly out and rub a drum when the line runs fast.

1 cell · cost 3

HOIST ⇑

fit-checked

A worm lifts a bucket up a mast on the lid, 2 mm a port turn, and holds it there with nothing to release. Teaches self-locking, a 20:1 ratio in one mesh, and the efficiency a worm costs.

1 cell · cost 9

Readouts

Show the line: dials, counters, sliders, plots.

METER ◔

fit-checked

Two cells tall: a dial for the row arriving on W in each cell, and the power line passed from S to N with its own dial.

1×2 cells · cost 2 (5 on the line)

TURNS #

fit-checked

Counts turns of the line to a tenth, 0.0 to 9.9, on two digit wheels under windows.

1 cell · cost 4

TRIG · WAVE sin

fit-checked

TRIG reads sin, cos and tan of one angle; WAVE draws the sine wave on a card, one wave per crank turn.

2×1 cells · cost 6 (TRIG), 4 (WAVE)

ORRERY ☉

fit-checked

Eight planets round the sun on nested tubes, one crank turn per Earth year. Speeds are compressed, not to scale.

3×3 cells · cost 4

TALLY #

fit-checked

Counts turns of the line to a tenth, 00.0 to 99.9, on three digit wheels under windows.

2×1 cells · cost 8

TOLL #

fit-checked

A counter that passes the line on: counts turns to a tenth, 0.0 to 9.9, and E turns with W, x1 forward.

1 cell · cost 4

PLOT ∿

fit-checked

A continuous plotter. A looping tread sweeps a green spot across the lid for time (W), and a crank pin carries the whole tread up and down for the signal (S), like an oscilloscope.

2×1 cells · cost 7 (W), 3 (S)

FACE ◷

fit-checked

A clock face with hour, minute and seconds hands that counts TICK's beats, one beat a second. Divides by 60, 60 and 12 like a real clock; push the minute hand to set it.

2×2 cells · cost 8

SKETCH ✎

fit-checked

An etch-a-sketch and pen plotter: two red knobs, or SX (south, west cell: x/time ÷4) and SY (south, east cell: y/signal ×2).

2×2 cells · cost 5 on each input

DYNAMO ↯

fit-checked

A speed lamp: W or S turns a small motor backward as a generator, and an LED in the lid lights, green forward and red back, brighter the faster you turn. No battery.

1 cell · cost 12 from S, 14 from W (first guess)

1 cell · cost 4 (5 to E)

A random junction: flick the wheel, lock it, and the line leaves on E or on N. The gear system's first "if".

Sound and rhythm

Turn the line into beats: taps, booms and programmed drums.

BEAT ♩

fit-checked

A drummer on a wire: a rhythm wheel lifts a hammer and drops it on a tok block, and the line goes on to E. Teaches frequency and rhythm.

1 cell · cost 3

BOOM ✹

fit-checked

Click, click, click… BOOM. A snail cam winds a band for one crank turn, then lets go into a drum. Teaches energy storage.

2×1 cells · cost 2 + wind-up

ROLL ♫

fit-checked

A programmable drum machine: a swappable peg disc plays a drum, a tok block and a tine, eight steps a bar. Teaches programs as data.

2×1 cells · cost 2 + keys
