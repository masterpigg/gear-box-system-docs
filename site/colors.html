Nine families, nine lids

The families are the sections of the catalog, plus the switch and clock boxes now in design and a new Chance section for SPIN, FORK and SHUFFLE. Every color is a Bambu PLA Basic or Matte filament. None is blue, orange, green or white, so no lid can be mistaken for a port, a display or an inner gear. The tiles are top-view mock-ups: blue gears come in, orange go out, green parts are displays.

Checked for color blindness

The same nine lids and five role colors, simulated for the three kinds of color blindness. Lids are the squares, roles the dots. For normal vision and the two common kinds (deuteranopia and protanopia), the closest two lids are still ΔE 13 apart in CAM02-UCS: Motion shapers against Readouts, both dark, and Ratios against Chance, both light. The closest lid to a role color is Routing yellow against the orange output gear, and those sit in different places: lid on top, port in the wall. Tritanopia is rare (about 1 in 10,000) and is the weak spot: there Drives red and Sound magenta come out close (ΔE 6), and so do Routing yellow and Chance pink (ΔE 10). Every lid also carries its name, so color is never the only cue.

Where the color goes

Four ways to color TEE, a routing box. The standard is a colored lid with a colored band round the top of the walls. Ports stay on bone, and the band carries the family round the sides of a box at the edge of a layout.

Printing on the P1S without an AMS

  • Lids: print a plate of one family's lids face up. In Bambu Studio, right-click the layer slider at 2.4 mm (where the raised text starts) and add a color change. The printer pauses, you swap to white or black, and it prints the lettering. This is the same single swap the black lettering needs today.
  • Band: add a color change on the shell at 19.2 mm, the bottom of gear layer 4. Everything above it is family color: the wall tops, the corner blocks and the tips of the fixed pins, which are hidden under the lid.
  • With an AMS the colored 3MF export does all of it by object; the lid object already has two colors.
  • A lid is about 10 g, so one 1 kg spool makes about a hundred lids. Corner caps were rejected because they add parts, and lid inlays because they need an AMS.

Lettering: dark lids get white, light lids get black

Your rule, made exact with WCAG relative luminance. White lettering comes from the inner-gear white filament and black from today's black, so no new lettering spool is needed. With those two, white and black give the same contrast at L = 0.18. Raised 5 mm Tahoma Bold counts as large text, so 3:1 is the floor.

  • Exception band: between L 0.15 and 0.25 both colors pass and the numbers are a coin toss. There, saturated colors (red, magenta) get white, because WCAG 2 under-rates white on saturated colors and white reads better on them by eye. That makes Sound magenta white even though black scores 4.1 against 3.5.
  • Dial and digit-wheel marks stay black on green, whatever the lid.
  • A part on a lid in the lid's own color prints white (the crank wheel above).

Proposed STANDARD.md text

The full spec is in docs/boxes/COLORS.md, following the one-place docs rule. This goes into STANDARD.md under "Colors" and the design thread applies it in lib/gbs.scad: a family table, gbs_lid_col(b) and a new gbs_ink_col(b), and the draft libraries switched over to them.

> ### Families
>
> Every box belongs to one family, named in `docs/boxes/INDEX.md`. The family colors the **lid**;
> shells, keepers, sleeves and caps stay bone, so the role colors (red drive, blue input, orange
> output, green display, white inner gears) read the same on every box. Family colors never use
> the role hues blue, orange, green or white; Drives keep red.
>
> | Family | Lid (Bambu PLA) | Lettering |
> |---|---|---|
> | Drives | Basic Red `#D63A2F` | white |
> | Routing | Basic Sunflower Yellow `#FEC600` | black |
> | Switches & clocks | Matte Dark Blue `#042F56` | white |
> | Ratios | Matte Lilac Purple `#AE96D4` | black |
> | Motion shapers | Matte Dark Chocolate `#4D3324` | white |
> | Readouts | Matte Charcoal `#1E1E1E` | white |
> | Chance | Matte Sakura Pink `#E8AFCF` | black |
> | Sound & rhythm | Basic Magenta `#EC008C` | white |
> | Side exploration | Basic Dark Gray `#545454` | white |
>
> A new family takes a color at least ΔE 12 (CAM02-UCS) from every family and role color under
> normal, deuteranope and protanope vision. Every shell carries a band in the family color above
> z = 19.2 mm (a filament change), never lower, so ports always sit on bone.
>
> ### Lettering
>
> Lid text, arrows and symbols are raised 0.6 mm, Tahoma Bold, at least 2.0 mm, in white (the
> inner-gear white) or black. Use white when the lid's relative luminance is below 0.18 and black
> above it; contrast must be at least 3:1. Between 0.15 and 0.25 both pass: use white on saturated
> colors (red, magenta). Dial and digit marks stay black on green. A part on a lid in the lid's
> own color (the crank wheel on a red lid) prints white instead.

Decided

Chance as its own family

Yes. SPIN, FORK and SHUFFLE move out of Drives, Routing and Readouts into a pink Chance section.

White crank wheel

Yes. On the red CRANK lid the wheel prints white, so it stands out from the lid.

Band by default

Every shell gets the band, one filament change at 19.2 mm. A plain bone shell still fits.
