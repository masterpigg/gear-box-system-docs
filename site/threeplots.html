One motor, three plots: square, triangle, sine

One MOTOR drives a square wave, a triangle wave and a sine wave, each on its own PLOT, all on one time base. Both drawings were checked with the sandbox's own rules: lanes, the power line, and the motor's budget of 15 stages. The tower on the right needs two new parts: BRANCH, and PLOT with its time input on the west.

Why the tower is so much smaller

SQUARE and RAMP put their level out on N. Today PLOT takes its signal on W and its time on S, under its east cell, so every level has to climb a row, turn east through a TAP, and the time line has to find its own way round to the far cell. That is where the WIREs, TURNs and TAPs go.

Swap PLOT's inputs and the level goes straight up into it: time comes in on W from the column, the signal comes up from the box underneath. A column of BUS and BRANCH beside the tower feeds every row.

Paul's idea, and the flip

Moving the time port onto PLOT's west side is the key. Keeping the signal there too would put two ports on one side (lanes A and C, 14 teeth at most), and the box to the west would need two outputs on one side, which no box has. So the signal moves to the south side of the west cell, where SQUARE, RAMP and TURN already deliver.

Inside PLOT that is a re-route: W runs east along layer 1 to T, and S meets R through one idler. The tread, frame and lid stay as they are.

The new parts

  • BRANCH: TAP with a north port. S in, E and N out, x1. TAP's C already sits where it meets N (TAP is TEE mirrored), so it is one more port gear. On main and fit-checked.
  • PLOT, time on the west: chosen 27 Oct. Inside, W climbs to layer 3 and runs east to T over the signal's gears; time now costs 7 stages, signal 3.
  • WIRE 2, WIRE 3, POLE 2, POLE 3: the long wires. They don't shrink this layout, but they cut its parts.

To compare: the etch-a-sketch thread built the same three waves around its SKETCH box, with a TAP, a TEE and the wave box under each SKETCH (its layout).

What the traces look like

SQUARE and RAMP move their level 5.86 mm. Into PLOT that turns the peg about 37°, so the square and triangle traces are about 5 mm tall and nearly straight-sided; the sine is 16 mm tall. Set R's peg east-west at mid-level when you assemble it (one tooth is 20°).

The level runs back and forth, so backlash shows in it. In the tower the level reaches PLOT in 3 meshes; in the staircase it takes 6.
